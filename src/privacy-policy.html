Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what information Pocketboost ("we", "us") collects when you visit https://pocketboost.org and how it is used.

Information we collect

We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect personal details like your name or address unless you email us. Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools may automatically record things like your browser type, device, approximate location, pages visited and the time of your visit.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and our partners use them to make the site work, understand how it's used, and show ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the site may not work properly afterwards.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We may use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we will ask for your consent before using cookies for personalised ads.

Affiliate links

Some links on Pocketboost are affiliate links, including links to SHEIN. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a commission. The affiliate network or store may place its own cookies to track the referral. We don't see your payment details or personal information on the store's website.

Third-party websites

When you leave Pocketboost for another site, that site's own privacy policy applies. We aren't responsible for how other sites handle your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, or to ask us to delete it. In South Africa this is covered by the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). To make a request, email us using the details on our contact page.

Children

Pocketboost is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Pocketboost may earn a commission when you use our links or codes to make a purchase. Always check the offer on the store’s website before you buy, because terms can change.
