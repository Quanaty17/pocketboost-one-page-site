How to find your size on SHEIN Curve

Sizing is the biggest worry when you buy plus-size clothes online, and it's the main reason people send things back. The size on the tag can differ from one brand to the next, and sometimes from one item to the next. So it helps to go by your measurements instead of the letter or number you normally wear.

Measure yourself first

Use a soft tape measure and stand up straight. Measure your bust at the fullest part, your waist at the narrowest part, and your hips at the widest part. Keep the tape snug but not tight, and write the numbers down in centimetres, since most size charts use cm.

Compare your numbers to the size chart

Every product page has its own size chart, so use that one instead of a general chart. For tops and dresses, start with your bust measurement. For trousers and skirts, look at your waist and hips. If your measurements land in two different sizes, it's usually safer to go for the bigger one, especially if the fabric doesn't stretch.

Use the reviews

Scroll to the reviews and look for people who include a photo and say what size they ordered. Comments like "runs small" or "true to size" really do help. Try to find someone with a similar build to yours, because their photo will show you how the item sits on a real body.

Think about the stretch

A stretchy fabric is a lot more forgiving than a stiff one. If you're between sizes and the item has no stretch, size up. If it's very stretchy, your usual size is probably fine.

Keep a note of what fits

Once you find something that fits well, write down the item and the size you ordered. Next time you shop, you'll have a good starting point instead of guessing again.
