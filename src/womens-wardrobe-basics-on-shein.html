Women's wardrobe basics to buy first on SHEIN

If you're new to ordering from SHEIN, I'd start with simple pieces that are easy to wear again and less risky when it comes to fit. That way, if you get a size wrong, you haven't lost much.

Plain tops and t-shirts

These go with almost everything. Choose neutral colours like black, white, grey or navy, and check the fabric details and reviews for thickness.

Stretchy bottoms

Trousers and jeans are harder to size online. Leggings, elastic-waist trousers or stretchy skirts are more forgiving, so they're a good place to begin.

One simple dress

A plain dress can work for work, church, lunch or a night out if you change the shoes and accessories. Look for one with a bit of stretch and good reviews with customer photos.

A layer for cooler days

A cardigan, blazer or light jacket makes your other pieces more useful. In winter it can be the difference between wearing something and leaving it in the cupboard.

Bags and accessories

Bags, belts and jewellery don't have sizing problems, so they're a low-risk way to freshen up what you already own.

Stick to a few colours

If everything you buy goes together, you'll get far more outfits out of fewer items. Before you order, read how to spot good quality clothes.
