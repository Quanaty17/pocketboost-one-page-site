How to shop on SHEIN without regrets

SHEIN has a huge range at low prices, which is exactly why it's easy to over-order and end up disappointed. I think a few small habits make a big difference, especially if you're shopping from outside the US or Europe.

Don't trust the model photo alone

Product photos are styled, and the clothes can look different in real life. Scroll down to the customer reviews and look for photos from real buyers. They show you the actual colour, fit and fabric.

Check the size chart, not just your usual size

Sizing between brands and even between items can vary a lot. Measure yourself and compare your measurements to the size chart on the product page. Reviews often say things like "runs small" or "size up", which is useful to know before you order.

Read the material and care details

Look at what the item is made of. Thin, stretchy or very cheap fabric may not suit what you have in mind, for example for work or colder weather.

Know the delivery and returns rules for your country

Delivery times, shipping costs and return conditions can differ by country, and they can change. Check the delivery and returns information in the shop for your location before you pay, so you aren't surprised later. Also keep in mind that orders from overseas can be subject to import duties or taxes in some countries.

Use a code, but check the total

A promo code is a nice bonus, but only if the final price is really lower. See our guide on using a promo code, and always look at the total before you confirm the order.

Set a budget

It's easy to fill a cart with small items that add up. Decide on a limit first, and remove anything you wouldn't be happy to keep if it arrived tomorrow.
