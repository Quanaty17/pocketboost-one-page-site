About Pocketboost

Pocketboost is a small site that helps online shoppers find promo codes and deals, mainly for SHEIN. I started it because I kept seeing coupon sites full of expired codes and pop-ups, and I wanted something simpler that tells you honestly what works and what doesn't.

I'm based in South Africa, so I know how it feels when a "worldwide" deal turns out not to apply to your country. That's why the guides here explain how codes work, where they can fail, and what to check before you pay.

How the site makes money

Pocketboost is a SHEIN affiliate partner. If you shop through one of our links, we may earn a small commission. It doesn't change the price you pay. We may also show ads on the site. Neither of these affects what we write in our guides.

A fair warning

Offers change without notice, and some only work for new customers or certain countries. Always confirm the final price on the store's website before you buy.
